# AI/ML Jobs Market Analysis 2022–2024

> Analysed 16,467 AI/ML job postings to uncover salary trends,
> hiring demand patterns, and experience-level gaps using 
> Python, SQL, and Power BI.

---

## Key findings

- Research Scientist is the highest paying entry-level AI role
- $71,706 average salary gap between Entry Level and Senior Level
- 2023 was the peak hiring year; demand moderated in 2024
- Remote roles pay less than on-site, contrary to popular belief
- Data Scientist is the fastest growing role from 2022 to 2024

## Business recommendations

1. Prioritise Research Scientist roles for competitive entry-level hiring
2. Create structured 3-year progression paths to retain junior AI talent
3. Shift hiring strategy from volume to specialisation post-2023
